Auto wallpapers is a dynamic and versatile app that refreshes your device’s look with wallpapers chosen from your own albums. It gives you a simple way to personalize your home screen and lock screen.

Dynamic Wallpaper Changer: Set wallpaper changes at a chosen time interval so your device stays fresh without manual work.

Home and Lock Screen Customization: Use one album for both screens or configure the home screen and lock screen independently.

Change on Lock and Unlock: Optionally change the home and lock-screen wallpapers whenever the phone is locked or unlocked.

Image Effects: Darken, blur, vignette, or apply grayscale effects to wallpapers so they match your visual preferences.

Folder Support: Organize wallpapers into folders and keep albums ready for automatic updates.

On-Device Storage: Wallpapers and settings remain on your device for quick access and reduced data usage.

Auto wallpapers is designed to keep wallpaper changes running after the app is closed, subject to Android and device battery-management policies.
